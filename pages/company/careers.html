Why work at Ditosis?

We are a remote-friendly, fast-growing team of engineers, researchers, and builders who care deeply about responsible AI. We value autonomy, craftsmanship, and long-term thinking.

  • Competitive compensation and meaningful equity
  • Remote-first with flexible hours
  • Annual learning and conference budget
  • Health, dental, and wellness benefits

Open roles

We’re actively hiring across engineering, research, product, and go-to-market. If you don’t see a perfect fit, send us a note anyway.

How to apply

Email your resume, links (GitHub, portfolio, publications), and a short note on why you’d like to work at Ditosis to careers@ditosis.com.
