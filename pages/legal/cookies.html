Cookie Policy

How and why Ditosis uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and products.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and improve your experience.

Types of cookies

We may use strictly necessary, functional, analytics, and optional cookies. Some features may not function properly without certain cookies.

Managing preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Where required, we will present a consent banner with granular options.
