Synthetic data quality guide

How we think about, measure, and continuously improve the quality of synthetic datasets.

Core principles

High-quality synthetic data should preserve the statistical properties of real data, respect privacy guarantees, and be tailored to your model’s objectives.

  • Distribution matching and calibration
  • Diversity and coverage of edge cases
  • Bias and leakage detection

Practical checklist

Before deploying a dataset, review its distributions, run model‑in‑the‑loop tests, and compare performance against your real‑world baseline.

Working with our team

Your Ditosis engagement includes quality review sessions where we walk through reports and iterate on generations together.
