FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Ditosis, synthetic data, and how engagements work.

What kinds of data can you generate?

We support text, image, audio, video, tabular, and multimodal datasets. If you have a specialized format, we can usually support it with a short scoping call.

How long does a typical project take?

Most pilot projects complete within 2–4 weeks from signed scope to delivered datasets and quality reports.

Can you work with regulated industries?

Yes. Our platform and processes are designed for privacy‑sensitive environments, and we support SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA‑aligned engagements.

How do we get started?

Submit the request form on the main site or email us at hello@ditosis.com. We’ll respond within 1–2 business days.
